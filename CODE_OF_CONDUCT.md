# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in BumpAlert welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the work and assume good intent.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Protect other people's privacy and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized comments, threats, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private security or contact channels. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
